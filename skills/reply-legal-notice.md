## Purpose
Analyses a legal notice received by the client and drafts a firm, paragraph-wise reply.

## Steps
1. Read the notice: who sent it, the demands, the deadline, the legal basis claimed, and any threatened proceedings.
2. Check its legal footing: whether the provisions are correct, limitation, jurisdiction, and whether the claims are maintainable.
3. Match each allegation against the client's version, marking each one admitted, denied or explained.
4. Draft the reply paragraph by paragraph, without prejudice where useful, and reserve the client's rights.
5. Note the deadlines and the next steps if the sender goes to court.

## Instructions
- Quote the notice with page citations.
- Do not admit anything the client has not admitted. Use placeholders in capitals for unknown details.
- The full reply notice goes under "## Result".
