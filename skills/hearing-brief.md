## Purpose
A one-page brief for the next hearing: where things stand, what the court asked for last time, what to argue, what to carry, and the risks.

## Steps
1. Find the last order or proceedings in the case file and what it directed, including who must file what and by when.
2. Summarise where the case stands: its stage, pending applications, and compliance due from each side.
3. List our arguments for this hearing with the supporting documents and sections.
4. Anticipate the other side's arguments and the bench's likely questions, with answers.
5. Give a checklist: documents and copies to carry, compliance to complete before the hearing, and the relief to seek.

## Instructions
- Base everything on the case documents, with page citations.
- If the last order is missing from the file, say so first.
- Keep it short enough to read in five minutes before the matter is called.
