## Purpose
Drafts a precise RTI application with clear, numbered requests that are hard to refuse, and explains what happens next.

## Steps
1. Identify the right public authority and its Public Information Officer.
2. Turn the need into specific, numbered requests for records, not questions of opinion.
3. Check the exemptions that could be used to refuse, and phrase the requests to avoid them.
4. Draft the application with the fee and payment mode, and with BPL details if they apply.
5. Explain the time limits for the reply and the first appeal.

## Instructions
- Cite RTI Act sections 6, 7, 8 and 19 from the sources.
- Fees differ between the Centre and the States, so mark the amount "(verify)".
- The full application goes under "## Result".
