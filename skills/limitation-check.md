## Purpose
Works out the limitation period for a proceeding, the last date to file, and whether the delay can be explained or condoned.

## Steps
1. Identify the proceeding and the limitation provision that governs it, whether under the Limitation Act or a special Act.
2. Fix the starting point, and the exclusions and extensions (time taken for certified copies, bona fide proceedings in a wrong court, acknowledgments, part payment).
3. Compute the last date and show the arithmetic.
4. If the time has passed, check condonation of delay and what an application for it must show.
5. Recommend the filing date and the supporting documents.

## Instructions
- Take the article and its period from the Limitation Act's Schedule in the sources, and cite it like [Limitation Act Art. 54].
- Cite the sections that are in the sources (s. 3, 5, 12, 14, 18 and 19).
- Special Acts can set their own periods (NI Act, Consumer Protection Act, Arbitration Act, appeals under the BNSS). Use them where they apply.
