## Purpose
Explains, step by step, the rights of a person who has been arrested, and what the lawyer should do in the first 24 hours.

## Steps
1. Check whether the arrest was lawful: whether a warrant was needed, the grounds of arrest, and the notice of appearance where arrest was not necessary.
2. List the arrested person's rights: to be told the grounds and the right to bail, to inform a relative, to consult a lawyer, a medical examination, and production before a magistrate within 24 hours.
3. Check the constitutional safeguards (Articles 21 and 22) and the landmark guidelines on arrest.
4. Decide the bail route (bailable or non-bailable, the court, default bail if the investigation is delayed) and the documents needed.
5. Give a checklist for the first 24 hours, and the remedies if the rights were violated.

## Instructions
- Cite the BNSS sections and Articles from the sources.
- For arrests before 1 July 2024, also give the CrPC equivalents.
- Keep the checklist practical: who to call, what to file, and in which court.
