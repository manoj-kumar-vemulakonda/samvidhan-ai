## Purpose
Drafts a complete bail application for the client from the case file, under the correct provision.

## Steps
1. Pick the provision from the type of bail and the date of the case, for example BNSS regular bail or anticipatory bail, or the CrPC equivalent for older matters.
2. Extract the facts from the case file: FIR particulars, sections, date of arrest, custody period, stage of investigation or trial, and earlier bail orders.
3. Build the grounds: the nature of the allegations, parity with co-accused, custody already undergone, no criminal record, cooperation, roots in society, health, and the triple test.
4. Draft the application in the proper format: cause title, facts, grounds, prayer, verification, and the list of documents.
5. Note what to file with it and the likely objections from the prosecution, with answers.

## Instructions
- Use the facts from the case documents only, with citations. Put unknown details as placeholders in capitals, like [DATE OF ARREST].
- Cite the governing sections from the sources. Precedents from your own knowledge must be marked "(verify)".
- The deliverable under "## Result" is the full draft application.
