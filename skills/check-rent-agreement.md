## Purpose
Reviews a rent or lease agreement clause by clause for our client, and flags risks and missing clauses.

## Steps
1. Identify the parties, the property, the term, the rent, the deposit and the escalation.
2. Check the formalities: stamp duty, registration (a lease for more than a year must be registered), and execution and witnesses.
3. Review the key clauses: lock-in, termination and notice, maintenance and repairs, subletting, use, and the deposit refund.
4. Flag clauses that are one-sided, unenforceable or missing, for our client's side.
5. Recommend changes, with suggested wording for the most important ones.

## Instructions
- Quote the clauses with page citations.
- Use the Transfer of Property Act and Registration Act sections from the sources.
- Of the State rent laws only Kerala's Rent Control Act is in the library; for other States, and for stamp duty rates, mark what you say "(verify)".
- End with a risk table: Clause | Risk (high/medium/low) | Suggested change.
